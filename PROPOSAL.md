# Product Proposal

## What is the product, and who uses it?
Overview
VeilBid is a kind of marketplace for NFTs. It lets people trade privately and securely. VeilBid uses intelligence to help with trading. It is built on a kind of blockchain called Midnight. This blockchain keeps everything. Peoples bids, identities and strategies are never shared.

VeilBid also lets computers trade on their own. These computers can make decisions without telling anyone what they are doing. They can prove that they are doing the thing without giving away any secrets.

## PROBLEM

Most NFT marketplaces are not private. They use blockchains that show everything. This means that:

* People can see what others are bidding

* Some people can cheat by bidding

* It is easy to see who owns what

* Artists do not always get paid what they are owed

This is a problem because serious traders and big companies want to keep their business private.

## SOLUTION

VeilBid has a layer that keeps everything private. This means that:

* Bids are always private

* Transactions are anonymous

* The outcome of auctions is verified without sharing any secrets

* Artists always get paid what they are owed

## WORKING MODEL

* People submit bids

* The outcome of auctions is determined using proofs

* There is a system to make sure everyone is honest

* People can buy things instantly without sharing any secrets

## WHY MIDNIGHT

Midnight is a blockchain that is good for private applications. It has:

* A special way of keeping private information separate

* A built-in way of verifying secrets

* A way of keeping tokens and NFTs private

* A way of sharing information when necessary

This makes it perfect for financial systems that need to be private.


## KEY DIFFERENCE

* VeilBid keeps bids forever

* Ownership and transactions are anonymous

* There is no way to cheat by bidding

* Artists always get paid what they are owed

* The system is fair. Can be proven to be fair

* VeilBid is a foundation for intelligence to trade privately

## MVP

* VeilBid will have contracts for private auctions

* There will be a system for bids

* The outcome of auctions will be determined using proofs

* There will be a way to buy things instantly without sharing any secrets

* There will be an interface for users

## AIM TO BUILD

VeilBid is the start of a new kind of marketplace. It will be private, intelligent and fair. Artificial intelligence will be able to trade on its own without sharing any secrets.
